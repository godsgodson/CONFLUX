THE PLAYBOOK

HOW THIS DESK READS CONFLUENCE
  1. 01

    DIRECTION

    The EMA 13 × EMA 40 cross is the trigger pair. 13 riding above 40 = bullish structure on that timeframe; under = bearish. It flips first on 1s and 1m, last on 1D — that sequence is the early warning.

  2. 02

    MACRO FILTER

    EMA 153 is the regime line. Price above it: longs only. Below it: shorts only. A daily close through 153 is a regime change, not a dip.

  3. 03

    FAIR VALUE

    VWAP is what the market actually paid, weighted by volume. Holding above it = buyers in control. Losing VWAP on several timeframes at once is the mean-reversion tell.

  4. 04

    LEVELS

    S/R lines mark swing pivots (×N = touches). Order blocks shade the origin of strong impulses — zones the market defends. The VPVR shows where volume actually traded; the POC line is the magnet price reverts to.

  5. 05

    CONFLUENCE

    Stack the odds. When 4+ of 6 timeframes agree, probability concentrates. The forecast cone is ±1σ realized volatility — a range price should stay inside ~68% of the time, not a target. Enter on pullbacks, never against the 1D line.

  6. 06

    CONTEXT

    The Macro Context Rail frames every trade: crowded funding warns of squeezes, ETF flows show institutional demand, prediction markets price the Fed and the CPI print before they happen, book imbalance shows where resting orders sit, and the event calendar tells you when volatility arrives. Trade the stack — with the tide, not against it.
